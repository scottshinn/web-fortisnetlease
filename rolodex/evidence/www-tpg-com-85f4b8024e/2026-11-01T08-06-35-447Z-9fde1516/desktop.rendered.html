Overview

Built for a distinctive approach

Through TPG’s family office roots, entrepreneurial heritage, and West Coast base, we have developed a differentiated approach to alternative investments based on innovation, organic growth, and a culture of openness and collaboration. Since our founding in 1992, we have developed a distinct position in the industry, with a broadly diversified set of investment strategies, including private equity, impact, credit, real estate, and market solutions.
